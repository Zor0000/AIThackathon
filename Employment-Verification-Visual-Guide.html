01 · Workflow

Four steps from request to report

Outreach is blocked until consent is recorded. A mismatch remains visible until HR records a rationale.

1 / REQUEST

Capture the claim

HR records candidate, prior employer, contact, job title, dates, and consent.

→
2 / RESPONSE

Ask narrowly

An approved employer contact confirms facts, declines, or does not respond.

→
3 / COMPARE

Flag exact differences

Code compares claimed and employer-provided values field by field.

→
4 / REVIEW

HR owns the outcome

HR clarifies, escalates, or closes with a rationale and report.

No response is an incomplete verification, not a negative result. VeriSure never generates a hire/reject decision.

04 · Guardrails

Boundaries that make the workflow trustworthy

Consent is a gate

The request cannot leave HR until the candidate's consent is recorded. A live integration needs policy and legal approval too.

Missing is not negative

A decline, timeout, or no response becomes incomplete and asks HR to follow the approved path.

Humans decide

Automation structures and compares facts. HR documents the rationale; it is never a hiring-decision engine.
