01 · Why this matters

Start with the problem

Set the context before opening the product.

The problem

Employment verification is often slow, fragmented, and difficult to audit.

  • Candidate claims and documents are reviewed separately
  • Former-employer responses are unstructured
  • Differences can become subjective decisions

Traditional approach

HR works across email, spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual notes.

  • Repeated follow-ups and manual data entry
  • No consistent question flow
  • Limited evidence traceability

VeriSure solution

A consent-led, evidence-based workflow that structures the process end to end.

  • Extract and compare approved employment facts
  • Verify through a guided employer questionnaire
  • Keep the final conclusion with HR

AI in the HR system
AI supports the workflow where unstructured information needs to become usable evidence. It does not make a hiring decision.
AI ASSISTANCE 1

Document understanding

For scanned documents or images, schema-constrained OCR can identify approved fields such as employer, title, dates, and ID.

→
AI ASSISTANCE 2

Response normalization

A natural-language verifier reply can be normalized into a structured answer, then checked by the same deterministic validator.

→
CONTROL LAYER

Rules and evidence

Validation, sender checks, comparison logic, idempotency, and audit events remain deterministic and traceable.

Human review gate

VeriSure compares the candidate evidence, extracted document facts, and former-employer response field by field. A mismatch is a prompt for HR review—not an automatic rejection or truth judgment.

VerifiedRequested facts match the recorded employer response.
Discrepancy foundA difference is highlighted for clarification or manual review.
Unable to verifyA decline or missing response is recorded as incomplete evidence, not as false information.
